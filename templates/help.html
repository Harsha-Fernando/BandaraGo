BEFORE YOU SET OUT

A little help for your day

Find your kind of place

Choose Explore places and select a category. Select All places or Clear filter to see everything again. Open a place to see photographs, visitor information, and its map.

Build your day

On a place page, select Add to my day. In My day plan, move stops up or down. Remove a stop or clear the entire plan through the confirmation page. Choose Keep my plan to cancel.

Your plan stays in this browser

No visitor account is needed. Cookies must be enabled to save the plan in your session. Clearing browser cookies or ending the session may remove it. Unpublished or deleted attractions disappear from the plan.

Read the map and distances

Drag to pan and use + or − to zoom. If tiles do not load, use the coordinates and OpenStreetMap link below the map. Distances are approximate straight-line distances from Bandaragama. Arrange your own travel and check current access and hours before setting out.

Attraction sources and photo credits are included under Good to know on each place page. Staff manage content through the administrator area.
